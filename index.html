Section 01 — Profile

About

Senior Software Quality Assurance Specialist with 4+ years of experience building robust, scalable testing strategies across web, mobile, and API platforms. I combine strong manual-testing fundamentals with end-to-end automation to help engineering teams ship reliable software, faster.

At Absoluit, I architect and execute comprehensive test strategies — functional, regression, and performance — across web and mobile products, improving defect identification by 30% and cutting release cycles by 20% through CI/CD-integrated automated suites. I design test plans and coverage matrices aligned to product requirements, lead and mentor a team of junior testers, and partner closely with developers and DevOps to catch issues early, reducing production defects by 25%.

My toolkit spans Selenium WebDriver, Cypress, Postman, Burp Suite, and OWASP ZAP for functional, API, and security testing; C#, NUnit, MSTest, and xUnit for automation frameworks; Azure DevOps and JIRA for defect tracking; and Docker, CI/CD, SQL Server, and MySQL for environment setup and data validation.

I've applied this across a diverse product portfolio — white-label ride-hailing platforms, a co-parenting app, a customer-support platform, an eSIM management app, and an internal test-case management system — covering everything from real-time tracking and payments to secure messaging and authentication. I'm the recipient of an "Excellence in Quality Assurance" award and an internal Hackathon win, and I'm passionate about shift-left testing and continuously raising the bar for software reliability.
